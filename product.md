# Product Specification & Context

## 1. Vision & Strategy
* **Core Value Proposition**: For product teams struggling to decide what's next in their roadmap and for design teams who need operational structure, Erika finds customer and operational problems and turns them into effective products and workflows.
* **Target Audience**: Software products and design recruiters looking for designers, product managers and researchers, as well as the hiring managers they are working with.
* **Success Metrics**: 
- Direct traffic volume to portfolio index page
- Time spent on individual case study pages

## 2. Core Features & Scope
* **Homepage**: A landing page with a summary about Erika and her product design and strategy experience.
* **Password protected portfolio collection page**: A list / collection of case studies that can be filtered by type; branding, product design, ux research, end-to-end  
* **Case Study page**: A cases study about a project pulled directly from Notion database
* **Primary Workflows**:
  * [Workflow A]: [Visitor lands on password protected portfolio collection -> Enters password -> Lands on collection page and filters type of case studies to see -> Selects case study -> Reviews desired case study]
  * [Workflow B]: [Visitor lands on homepage -> Scrolls and reads about Erika]
